# MUNI Claim Policy

CANDIDATE -> FORMAL_PARTIAL -> VERIFIED_SEMANTIC -> VERIFIED_NATIVE -> MEASURED_LOCAL_NATIVE -> ADVERSARIAL_VERIFIED -> CROSS_ENVIRONMENT_VERIFIED.

UNIVERSAL_CLAIM is reserved for an actual universal proof. Contradictory or insufficient evidence is QUARANTINED.

A benchmark multiplier cannot promote itself to a universal theorem. Speedup guarantees require a proven cost-model bound or an explicitly bounded hardware model.
