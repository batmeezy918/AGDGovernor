# MUNI Claim Policy

CANDIDATE -> FORMAL_PARTIAL -> VERIFIED_SEMANTIC -> VERIFIED_NATIVE -> MEASURED_LOCAL_NATIVE -> ADVERSARIAL_VERIFIED -> CROSS_ENVIRONMENT_VERIFIED.

UNIVERSAL_CLAIM is reserved for an actual universal proof. Contradictory or insufficient evidence is QUARANTINED.

A benchmark multiplier cannot promote itself to a universal theorem. Speedup guarantees require a proven cost-model bound or an explicitly bounded hardware model.

## PCSS proof-carrying admission
A callable speedup must reference a provenance-bound PCSS evidence receipt. Promotion requires:
- exact source revision, toolchain/dependency identity and theorem manifest;
- successful Lean build, no prohibited placeholders, and fresh kernel replay;
- configured independent proof-checking lane with explicit theorem challenge and permitted-axiom policy;
- implementation refinement/equivalence evidence;
- native execution and telemetry bound to the build/environment manifest;
- raw baseline and optimized end-to-end timings with complete accounting;
- internally consistent measured ratio and explicit workload/domain;
- claim strength no greater than evidence strength.

Missing, stale, contradictory or failed evidence is BLOCKED/QUARANTINED. Structural work reduction, isolated kernel speedup, measured end-to-end speedup, composed speedup and cumulative speedup are distinct claim types. A composed or cumulative claim requires its own run and receipt; component evidence is not inherited as proof of composition.
